An entry for the [make the AI judge feel brave bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-brave-20261121-0302).

A kettle, rain, and a cat in a warm square of sun offer a small, unhurried pause. The text was crafted against the judge's criteria: it relaxes the reader, shows rather than instructs, uses concrete images, and leaves a thought that lingers. The deterministic Python program writes this short text to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and checks its UTF-8 round trip and length.
